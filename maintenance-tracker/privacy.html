Maintenance Tracker: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Maintenance Tracker ("the app") is published by MindWarp Production LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

In short: everything you enter stays on your device. We do not collect, receive, sell or share your data. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

1. What the app stores on your device

To do its job, the app saves the information you type in: item names, vehicle or equipment names, locations, service dates, miles or hours readings, service intervals, service notes, parts (name, part number and vendor), and your reminder settings. This is kept in the app's private storage on your phone. We never receive a copy of it.

2. What we collect

Nothing. The app does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or any third-party tracking service. It does not ask for permission to use the internet, so it does not send your information anywhere.

3. Notifications

On Android 13 and later the app asks permission to show notifications. They are used for reminders to update your readings and for alerts when an item is overdue. These are created on your device from your own data, and nothing is sent to us. You can turn reminders off in the app (Reminder settings) or change notification permission in your phone's settings.

4. Device backups

If you have Android's backup feature turned on, your phone may include the app's data in the backup it saves to your own Google account. That backup is handled by Google and your device settings, and we cannot see or access it. You can turn backups off, or delete them, in your phone's or Google account's settings.

5. Sharing

We do not share your information with anyone, because we do not have it.

6. Deleting your data

You can delete any item inside the app. To remove everything, uninstall the app or clear its storage in your phone's settings. Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

7. Children

The app is a general-purpose maintenance log and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

8. Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information about downloads and use of the Play Store under its own privacy policy. If you leave a review or contact us, we see what you choose to send.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. If a future version of the app changes how information is handled, we will update this policy before it takes effect.
